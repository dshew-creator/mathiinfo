What is MATHi

Not a knock-off.
Something of its own.

Standard curriculum was designed for the statistical middle. MATHi was designed from the ground up for the students those systems consistently leave behind teenagers with Dyscalculia, ADHD, and learning differences who can think deeply but don't learn linearly.

MATHi wraps business-oriented algebra inside the worlds students already love. Star Wars. Barbie. Formula 1. Gaming. The math doesn't change but the student's relationship to it does.

Every feature from Shaper to the Imaginator visual engine is structural. Not decoration. It's how the learning happens.

The History of MATHi

Introduced to LMU & the World

From a personal struggle to a breakthrough in educational technology MATHi was built out of lived experience and presented to the world at Loyola Marymount University's 18th Undergraduate Research Symposium in March 2026.

🎓 LMU 18th Undergraduate Research Symposium · March 2026

The Problem Identified
Daniel Shew a Loyola Marymount University student, Class of 2029 growing up with Dyscalculia and ADHD, experienced firsthand how standard math curriculum fails students with learning differences.

MATHi is Built
Designed for late-middle to early-high school teenagers, MATHi teaches business-oriented algebra through familiar franchise narratives connecting math to students' personal interests and adapting to their individual learning mindset.

Research & Validation
14 interviews conducted with University and High School students, international adult representatives from 7 countries, and university faculty who grew up with learning disabilities confirming the gap MATHi was built to fill.

March 2026 World Debut
MATHi was officially presented at LMU's 18th Undergraduate Research Symposium, introducing a new paradigm for how students with Dyscalculia and ADHD engage with mathematics.

Core Features

Every feature is structural not cosmetic.

Each element addresses a specific barrier students with learning differences face every day.

🧠
The Imaginator

Turns word problems into interactive visual scenes. Students manipulate the actual objects in the problem bounty targets, Dreamhouse rooms, race laps and feel the equation before solving it.

✦
Shaper

MATHi's mascot manages attention, celebrates wins, and creates a non-threatening environment throughout. The emotional architecture built around how anxious learners actually process.

🃏
Flashcard Formula

After solving, students see a plain-language step-by-step breakdown matched to their franchise world. Concept over memorization understanding first, answer second.

🌍
Franchise Worlds

Six fully themed environments Star Wars, Barbie, Hot Wheels, Videogames, Music Industry, Formula 1. Every problem and scene is built around the student's world.

🎯
Got It Gate

Students confirm they understand the concept not just that they got the answer before moving on. Concept confidence over problem completion, always.

🚀
Career Connections

Every concept unlocked links to real careers Mechanical Engineers, Racing Analysts, VFX artists, Game Designers. Students see the math in a future they actually want.

About the Founder

Built from lived experience.

Daniel Shew

Founder of MATHi · LMU Class of 2029

Daniel Shew grew up with speech delay, Dyscalculia, and ADHD three conditions that shaped his relationship with learning from a very early age. Mathematical processing wasn't just difficult; it felt like a wall that school never helped him get through, only around.

Watching standard curriculum continue to fail students who learned the way he did, Daniel set out to build something different. Not an accommodation. Not a modified textbook. A fundamentally new architecture for how math is taught to teenagers with learning differences.

As a student at Loyola Marymount University's College of Business, he channeled that experience into MATHi an AI-powered learning environment that meets students where they actually are, using the media and franchise worlds they already care about to make algebra feel like something that belongs to them.

MATHi is Daniel's answer to the question he kept asking as a kid: "Why does everyone else seem to get this?" The answer, it turns out, wasn't about intelligence. It was about how the learning was delivered.
